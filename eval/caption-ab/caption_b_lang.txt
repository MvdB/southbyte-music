Global Metadata
Basic Attributes: bpm is 150. key is E, and scale is minor. Melodic Heavy Metal / Symphonic Metal.
Global Emotional Progression: The track is defiant and forward-leaning from the opening bar. The verse holds its power in reserve, tightly coiled and rhythmically clipped, while the pre-chorus lifts the harmonic floor and shortens the phrasing to signal what is coming. The chorus releases into wide, anthemic triumph without losing the underlying urgency.
Application Scenarios & Imagery: Standing ground against something larger than yourself; machinery humming under a night sky; a decision made and not taken back.
Sonics & Production Profile: A wide, dense stereo image with hard-panned rhythm guitars and the orchestral layer seated clearly behind them rather than beside them. Upper mids are bright and cutting so guitars and lead vocal stay legible through the density; the low end is tight and punchy rather than boomy. Loud and heavily compressed in the modern metal manner, but the intro is left comparatively open so the first full-band entry lands as a genuine jump in level.

Vocal Details
Vocal Gender & Timbre: A single female lead vocalist, clean throughout, with a powerful and bright timbre that carries over dense instrumentation without straining. Warm and controlled in the lower register, ringing and resonant at the top.
Vocal Style: Restrained and rhythmically precise in the verse, sitting close to the riff. The pre-chorus opens the phrasing and raises the register step by step. The chorus is a full-throated belt with sustained notes across the line ends, projected rather than shouted, and never breaking into harsh or screamed delivery.
Harmony/Backing Vocals: No harmonies in the verse. Thin octave doubling enters in the pre-chorus. The chorus carries multi-tracked self-harmonies in thirds and fifths, wide-panned, thickening the hook into a near-choral texture.
Vocal FX: Plate reverb of moderate length for size without smearing the consonants, a short slap delay on line ends in the verse, and a touch of saturation on the chorus lead so it cuts through the guitar wall.

Arrangement
Intro: Orchestral strings and a low sustained pad establish the key alone, spacious and unhurried, with a single rising cymbal swell leading in.
Verse: Full band enters on the downbeat. Heavily distorted rhythm guitars take over as the harmonic engine with fast palm-muted chugging; bass locks to the kick; drums run a tight double-kick pattern with sharp snare accents. Strings drop back to a quiet sustained bed. Vocal sits forward and dry-ish.
Pre-Chorus: Palm muting opens into ringing chords. The string section climbs in register and volume, orchestral percussion adds weight underneath, and a riser plus reverse cymbal carries the last two bars into the release.
Chorus: Guitars go to open, sustained power chords; crash cymbals mark every downbeat; the full string section swells beneath the guitars rather than over them, doubling the vocal contour an octave up. Bass and kick stay locked to hold the low end tight under the widened top.
Outro: The band cuts and the strings are left to decay, ending on a sustained chord that thins rather than fades.
